# Scoutly first-sale traffic playbook

Public site: https://tiru67.github.io/scoutly-price-scout-privacy/

## Before posting

- Use Amazon-generated or SiteStripe links when exact product pages are available.
- Keep the disclosure near every affiliate CTA.
- Say “check current Amazon.in price”; do not publish an invented live price.
- Post where the topic is relevant and promotion is permitted.

## Three posts to publish this week

### Post 1 — power banks

Buying a power bank? Capacity is not enough. Check USB-C output wattage, safety information, warranty, seller reputation, and the delivered price. Scoutly’s checklist: https://tiru67.github.io/scoutly-price-scout-privacy/guides/best-power-banks-under-2000-india.html

### Post 2 — headphones

Before buying wireless headphones, compare the exact model code, microphone quality, battery life with ANC enabled, comfort, warranty, and return terms. Guide: https://tiru67.github.io/scoutly-price-scout-privacy/guides/best-wireless-headphones-under-3000-india.html

### Post 3 — routers

A router’s advertised speed will not fix an incompatible ISP setup or poor placement. Check connection type, coverage, ports, firmware support, and the cost of a complete mesh kit. Guide: https://tiru67.github.io/scoutly-price-scout-privacy/guides/best-wifi-routers-for-home-india.html

## 30-day rhythm

- Publish 3 useful short videos per week.
- Share one guide per week in a relevant community where links are allowed.
- Ask one knowledgeable friend to review each guide for accuracy.
- Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console.
- Track outbound Amazon clicks and improve the page with the most engagement.

## Do not do

- Do not buy through your own links.
- Do not ask friends to place artificial orders.
- Do not spam Reddit, WhatsApp, Facebook, or comments.
- Do not claim Scoutly found the lowest price unless a verified source actually supports that claim.
