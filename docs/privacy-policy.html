Scoutly Price Scout Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

What Scoutly does

Scoutly Price Scout detects product information on the active shopping page and helps users compare prices before purchasing.

Information processed

When you click the Scoutly extension icon, it may read the product title, displayed price, currency, product image, page URL, and website hostname from the active page. This is used only to show the detected product and create comparison searches.

Storage and sharing

The current version processes product information locally in your browser. Saved price-watch items are stored in Chrome storage on your device. Scoutly does not sell, rent, or share this information and does not send it to a remote server in the current MVP.

Information not accessed

Scoutly does not read passwords, payment details, checkout form data, private messages, or information from pages unless you explicitly click the extension while viewing that page.

Retention and deletion

Saved watches remain in your browser until you remove them or clear the extension’s storage. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data according to Chrome’s storage behavior.
