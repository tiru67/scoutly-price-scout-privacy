ABOUT SCOUTLY

Shopping research for better decisions.

Scoutly is an independent shopping-research project for Indian buyers. We focus on the details that are easy to miss when shopping quickly: exact model identity, final delivered cost, seller quality, return terms, warranty, and whether a discount is actually unusual.

Our approach

Our guides are written to be useful even when a reader does not use the Scoutly extension. We explain what to compare, which product claims need verification, and when the cheapest listing is not the best purchase.

Our technology

Scoutly is building a browser extension that can identify products and compare source-backed offers through approved retailer APIs. We will label connected sources and retrieval times clearly. We do not present an estimated or invented price as a live offer.
