ABOUT SCOUTLY

Shopping research for better decisions.

Scoutly is an independent shopping-research project for Indian buyers. We focus on the details that disappear when shopping gets rushed: exact product identity, current price evidence, seller context, warranty and the real conditions behind a discount.

What Scoutly is building

The site turns product research into decision-ready guides that humans and shopping agents can understand. The Scoutly browser extension is being developed to identify the current product page and return source-backed comparisons instead of sending shoppers to a generic search.

Who writes and checks the guides

Guides are published by the Scoutly Editorial Desk. Each commercial guide names its retrieval time, source pages, merchant scope, affiliate relationship and whether the products were physically tested. Material updates appear in an update log.

Merchant coverage

Scoutly currently researches official brand stores, specific Amazon.in listings when independently accessible, and supported EarnKaro partner routes. Coverage varies by guide and is never represented as the entire Indian market unless the merchant set is explicitly complete.

How recommendations earn trust

A recommendation should survive four questions: Is it the exact product? Is the price current? Is the buying condition clear? Is this option right for the reader's use case? Read the complete verification and editorial methodology.
