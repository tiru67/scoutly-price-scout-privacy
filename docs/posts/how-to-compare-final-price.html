PRICE LITERACY

How to compare the real final price online

The bold number on a product card is rarely the complete cost. A fair comparison uses the same quantity, variant, delivery destination, and purchase conditions.

Use a five-part total

Write down item price, shipping, taxes or fees, coupon savings, and cashback. If a site shows a discount before the cart, verify that it applies to the exact variant you want. Compare the total payable amount, not the crossed-out list price.

Check like-for-like details

A 2-pack is not the same as a single item. Storage capacity, colour, size, seller, warranty, and import status can all change the value. Save the model number or ASIN when available.

Record the evidence

Note the price, seller, delivery estimate, and retrieval time. Prices can change between research and checkout. A good comparison tells you what was checked and when.

Bottom line

The best offer is the lowest trustworthy total for the exact product you want—not the lowest number found in a search result.
