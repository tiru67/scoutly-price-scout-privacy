Best Wi‑Fi routers for home in India

A router should match your broadband connection, home layout, devices, and support expectations—not just its headline speed.

Start with your connection

Confirm whether your provider supplies a modem or optical network terminal and whether the router supports the required connection type. A fast wireless standard cannot fix an incompatible setup.

For a typical apartment

Compare dual-band Wi‑Fi, app setup, firmware support, and the return policy. Placement can matter as much as the advertised range.

For a larger or multi-floor home

Consider a mesh system if walls and floor plans make one-router coverage unreliable. Compare the cost of the full set, not only one node.

Final checklist

Check ports, warranty, firmware reputation, parental controls if needed, and whether the seller is trustworthy. The right router is the one that works with your connection and remains supportable.
