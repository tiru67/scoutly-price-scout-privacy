PRIVACY POLICY

Scoutly Price Scout

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Scoutly Price Scout helps shoppers detect product information on the active shopping page and compare prices before purchasing.

Information the extension processes

When you click the Scoutly extension icon, Scoutly may read the product title, displayed price, currency, product identifiers, product image, page URL, and website hostname from the active page. The product title and identifiers are sent to Scoutly’s configured comparison backend to search connected retailer APIs and return source-backed price candidates.

How information is stored and shared

Saved price-watch items are stored in Chrome storage on your device. Product information needed for a comparison is sent to Scoutly’s comparison backend and connected retailer APIs. Scoutly does not sell or rent this information.

Information Scoutly does not access

Scoutly does not read passwords, payment details, checkout form data, private messages, or information from pages unless you explicitly click the extension while viewing that page.

Data retention and deletion

Saved watches remain in your browser until you remove them through the extension or clear the extension’s storage. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data according to Chrome’s storage behavior.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the extension’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new revision date.
