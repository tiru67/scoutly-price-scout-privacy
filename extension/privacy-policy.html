Scoutly Price Scout Privacy Policy

Scoutly Price Scout reads product title, price, currency, product identifiers when available, and page URL from the active page after the user clicks the extension. The product title and identifiers are sent to Scoutly's configured comparison backend to search connected retailer APIs and return source-backed price candidates. Saved watches are stored locally in Chrome storage. We do not read form inputs, payment details, passwords, or checkout contents.
